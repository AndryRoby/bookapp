What it does

The core idea: your shelves are modelled in real millimetres, and books sit on them the way they actually sit — spine out, flat, cover forward, or turned in depth.

Scan the ISBN

Point your phone's camera at the barcode — one at a time or in a fast burst — and bookapp fills in the title, author and cover automatically.

Real-size shelves

Every shelf, cell, cabinet and drawer is drawn to its true millimetre dimensions, not a generic grid — the same way a spatial twin of your furniture would be.

Know what fits, and where

Before you buy a book or a new bookcase, see whether it fits and exactly which shelf and cell it belongs on.

Sync phone and PC over Wi-Fi

Your phone and your Windows PC exchange the library directly over your local network — no server, no cloud, you choose the sync direction.

Works offline

Adding books, browsing shelves, tracking reading — none of it needs an internet connection.

No account, ever

Your library lives on your device. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to lose access to.

What it does not do yet

Said plainly, because we'd rather you know before you download.

  • No automatic measurement from a photo. We tried this seriously — several different approaches, tested on real photos of real shelves, all documented. None were accurate enough to trust, so bookapp doesn't pretend to do it. Instead, you either measure a book once with a ruler, or pick its format (paperback, hardback, etc.) and bookapp marks that thickness as approximate until you correct it.
  • No iPhone or iPad app. bookapp currently runs on Android and Windows only.
  • Not on Google Play or the Microsoft Store. This is a direct-download beta while we work out the basics — licensing, signing — properly, rather than rush a store listing.

Nothing here is a "coming soon" promise. If any of it changes, this page will say so plainly, and not a moment before it's true.

Installing on Android

bookapp isn't on Google Play yet, so Android will warn about installing from "unknown sources." That's expected for a direct-download beta — allow it for this one file, then open the downloaded APK to install.

Installing on Windows

The build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen will likely show "Windows protected your PC." Click "More info," then "Run anyway." Unzip the folder first and run bookapp.exe from inside it.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. bookapp is a free public beta for Android and Windows — no trial limits, no paywall, no in-app purchases. We haven't decided if or how a paid version will exist later.

Where is my data stored?

On your own device, in a local database bookapp creates. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. If you sync a phone and a PC, the data moves directly between them over your local Wi-Fi, never through a server.

Does it need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no login and no email required to use the app.

How do I measure books?

Either measure a book once with a ruler and type in the millimetres, or pick its format (paperback, hardback, etc.) and bookapp uses a typical thickness for that format, always labelled as approximate. bookapp does not measure books automatically from a photo; we tested that seriously and it wasn't reliable, so we don't ship it.

Does it work on iPhone?

No, not yet. bookapp currently runs on Android and Windows only.

How do I sync phone and PC?

Open bookapp on both devices while they're on the same Wi-Fi network, start a sync and choose which direction to merge. The two devices talk to each other directly over the local network; no cloud service is involved.
